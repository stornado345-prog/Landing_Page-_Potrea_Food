How It Works

A simple 4-step ordering process designed for quick and effortless self-service dining.

01

Choose Menu

Browse available food and drinks by category with photos, descriptions, and pricing.

02

Add to Cart

Select products and quantities with optional notes or special instructions for the kitchen.

03

Confirm Order

Review the order summary, select payment method, and confirm the ticket with one tap.

04

Track Order

Receive an order number and monitor the order status in real time as the kitchen prepares your meal.

Empowering F&B Businesses

Made for Restaurants & UMKM

Poetra Food is a self-ordering restaurant solution built specifically for culinary businesses and UMKM (micro, small, and medium enterprises). Unlike complex food delivery marketplaces, Poetra Food focuses on streamlining the in-house dining experience.

By replacing paper menus and verbal order-taking with an intuitive Android tablet or kiosk interface, restaurants can serve more guests, avoid costly ordering mistakes, and maintain smooth operations during peak rush hours.

Built for seamless offline-first stability, low hardware requirements, and minimal staff training.
